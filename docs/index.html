I am Haoze Yu (于浩泽), a Computer Science and Technology undergraduate at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITsz), with plans to continue my academic journey as an incoming Ph.D. student at Tsinghua University.

My research explores the intersection of generative modeling, physical reasoning, multimodal understanding, and AI safety. I am especially interested in building models that can represent interactions between objects, edit multiple modalities precisely, and preserve consistency beyond surface-level realism.

I enjoy connecting theoretical questions with reproducible experiments—designing evaluation protocols, implementing models, and turning ideas into reliable research systems.

Current research directions

SEP 2026 — PRESENT
R / 01

Object-Centric, Action-Controllable Generative World Models

Investigating how an action affects multiple interacting objects—and how that influence propagates through a scene. The research explores dynamic interaction graphs, graph neural networks, action-effect gating, paired trajectory supervision, and multi-step rollout training, with Pymunk-based simulated environments for controlled evaluation.

Interaction graphsGNNsAction-effect gatingPhysical consistency
2026 — NOW
R / 02

Compositional Audio-Visual Editing with Disentangled Representations

Exploring how to separate shared content from visual and acoustic style so that edits stay local, precise, and consistent. The work investigates dual-stream encoders, contrastive representation learning, and conditional generation for visual-only, audio-only, and joint editing.

Contrastive learningDisentanglementPrecise editingNon-target preservation
